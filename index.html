About Me

Hello! I’m a Computer Science student at Effat University. I’m currently in my third year, focusing on my university projects, real-world problem solving, innovation, and sustainability. I’m passionate about using technology to find practical solutions to real-life challenges and create innovative ideas that can contribute to a better and more sustainable future.

The rapid evolution of AI and its ability to solve problems in new and creative ways fascinated me. Since then, I’ve been exploring how technology and AI can be used to find smarter solutions to real-world problems, while continuously learning and looking for ways to contribute to a better and more sustainable future.
